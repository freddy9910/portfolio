Custom application

Bless Dental Clinic

A clinic management system written from scratch in PHP. No CMS underneath it — an application built around how the practice actually runs, behind a staff login, entirely in Arabic and right-to-left.

Ten modules cover the working day: a patient register and per-patient file, a visits log, bookings, case follow-ups, doctor reports, services, and the financial accounts. The dashboard opens on live figures — patient count, today’s visits and revenue, outstanding balances, the week’s bookings — over a seven-day revenue chart. The public site in front of it carries treatments and online consultation booking.
